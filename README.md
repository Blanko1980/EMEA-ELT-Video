# THE LAST FOUR YEARS
*A brief history of the AI era.* An opening film for the EMEA ELT 2026.

A ~67-second cinematic sequence in a single file, `index.html`: canvas motion, typography and a generative Web Audio score. There's no video file, no audio file and no build step.

## Present it
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. It needs an internet connection the first time to load the Libre Baskerville and Inter fonts. Without one, it falls back to system fonts.
2. Click the play ring. The browser goes fullscreen, the title fades and the film rolls with sound.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space / Enter | Play / pause |
| F | Fullscreen |
| M | Mute |
| R | Restart |
| ← / → | Skip 5 s back / forward |

Move the mouse to show the progress bar, which you can click to scrub. `index.html?t=42` opens paused at 42 s for rehearsal, and `?debug` shows tenths of a second.

## Structure
| Time | Scene | Line on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–9 s | A cursor, a chat window, a poem floods the screen | NOVEMBER 2022 · WE THOUGHT IT WAS A TOY. |
| 9–17 s | Windows multiply into thousands, become circles, then a network | 2023 · WE STARTED EXPERIMENTING. |
| 17–27 s | The network becomes a hub: presentations, video, design, code, search, translation | 2024 · WE STOPPED ASKING IF AI MATTERED. |
| 27–37 s | The camera pulls back: cities, transport, medicine, education, finance, communication | 2025 · AI BECAME INFRASTRUCTURE. |
| 37–47 s | A living ecosystem of identity rings, accelerating | agents · automation · networks · knowledge · decisions · 2026 · AI IS EVERYWHERE. |
| 47–52.5 s | Maximum speed: everything becomes one node of a much larger network. Hard stop at 51 s, then silence. | — |
| 52.5–61 s | Silence, then two lines | THE STORY IS JUST BEGINNING. · THE NEXT FOUR YEARS START NOW. |
| 61–67.6 s | A canary seed opens into Deep Plum, with rotating concentric rings and a warm D-major chord | EMEA ELT 2026 · HUMANIZE COMPLEXITY |

The camera is one continuous pull-back: each era becomes the centre of the next.
